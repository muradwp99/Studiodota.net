# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective architecture clients — private homeowners, property developers, and institutions — plus collaborators and press evaluating Studiodota's work. They arrive to judge taste, rigor, and track record (mostly desktop, often mobile) and want to *feel* the quality of the practice before making contact.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and portfolio website for **Studiodota, a real architecture & design practice**. It communicates the studio's point of view, showcases projects, and converts interest into an enquiry. Success = a visitor leaves convinced the studio is precise, human, and premium — and reaches the contact form.

## Brand Personality

Precise, human, enduring. Confident but calm — restraint over noise. Editorial and architectural: generous space, strong typography, cinematic imagery. The voice is plainspoken and assured, never salesy or hype-driven.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero-metric templates, identical icon-card grids, gradient-on-white).
- Cluttered real-estate listing portals (dense, stocky, transactional).
- Loud startup neon / decorative gradients and "AI-made" sameness.
- Over-animated one-pagers where motion is decoration rather than meaning.

## Design Principles

- **Let the architecture lead** — full-bleed imagery and cinematic scale; quiet UI around it.
- **Clarity and restraint** — every element earns its place; generous negative space.
- **Motion with intent** — scroll and transitions reveal and guide, never distract; always reduced-motion safe.
- **One coherent identity** — champagne-bronze on warm paper, Poppins display, adaptive glass nav; consistent voice across sections even when treatment varies.
- **Accessible by default** — keyboard operability, contrast, and reduced motion are requirements, not extras.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Keyboard-operable navigation and controls with visible focus; body contrast ≥4.5:1 (≥3:1 large text); complete `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks across all Lenis/GSAP/Framer motion. Light and dark themes are both maintained.
